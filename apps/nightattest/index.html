What the circuit reveals

Private during presentation
The holder secret, selected commitment and Merkle path. The circuit checks membership against the current active root.
Public on a deployed contract
Issuance and revocation expose commitments, slots and status. A presentation reveals its challenge, the root checked and acceptance. Transaction timing and metadata can still reveal patterns.
